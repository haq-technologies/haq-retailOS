# Shop visit checklist (Phase 0)

For the field lead's first visit to the pilot shop. Allow half a day, including a busy period. Afterwards the results go into PLAN.md (open items) and docs/decisions.md.

## Bring

- The catalogue workbook, docs/catalogue/starter-catalogue-v1.1.xlsx, printed or on a laptop, open at the Changes, Categories, Brands and Phone models tabs.
- A laptop with a USB barcode scanner and Notepad open, a phone for photos, and a stopwatch.
- This checklist.

## 1. With the owner (about 45 minutes)

- [ ] Walk through the Changes tab and get catalogue v1.1 re-confirmed, noting every correction.
- [ ] Read out the example printed names on the Categories tab, and check each reads the way the shop says it ("iPhone 11 Silicone Pouch").
- [ ] Ask whether screen protectors should print as "Tecno Spark 20 Tempered Glass Screen Protector", and whether "car mp3 s8" may print as "Car MP3 S8".
- [ ] Confirm prices: the owner and the manager set them, and one tap turns the first price typed for an item into its set price.
- [ ] Ask which counters take money, and whether each one wants a cash drawer.
- [ ] Ask whether the manager may enter cost prices (a console setting), and book two 30-minute cost sessions a week.
- [ ] Ask what the shop does today when the PC or the power fails.

## 2. Shelf walk (about 60 minutes)

- [ ] For each category, list the brands on the shelf, especially for wireless chargers, phone holders, memory cards and flash drives, batteries, selfie sticks and ring lights, and computer accessories (C8). Photograph each shelf.
- [ ] Confirm or strike the 16 brands marked "To confirm on the shelf walk", and the short codes HF, AI, LP, BM, DV, JZ, EX and UWO.
- [ ] Find J7, car mp3 s8, Pro and Pro 5s on the shelf, and note what each one is.
- [ ] For pouches, screen protectors and batteries, note the phone models in stock, and mark any missing from the Phone models tab.

## 3. Barcode test (about 20 minutes)

- [ ] Pick 20 items across the categories. For each, record whether it has a barcode, whether it scans, and whether the same code appears on a different product.
- [ ] The spec assumes 40% scannable; write down the real share.

## 4. Watch a busy period (about 2 hours)

- [ ] Time 20 sales, from the first item to paid, as today's baseline.
- [ ] Note the payment mix (cash, transfer, POS card), how each transfer is confirmed, and how long that takes.
- [ ] Note how many cashiers and counters work at once, where the cash is kept, and whether receipts are printed.

## 5. Count sales from the notebook

- [ ] Count the sales for each of the last 7 days, and note the busiest day and hour.

## 6. Power and network survey

- [ ] Mark where the server, switch, counters and printers will go, and the cable runs between them.
- [ ] Note what the inverter or solar system powers, and for how long.
- [ ] Check the phone signal where the server will stand, for the update hotspot.

## After the visit

- [ ] Send the catalogue corrections to Claude to regenerate v1.1, and record the answers in docs/decisions.md and PLAN.md.
